🌐 English SummaryEN brief

KOSPI closed +1.76% at 5,768.01, KOSDAQ +1.78% at 808.89, both reversing a sharp morning drop (KOSPI opened down 3.17% at 5,488.41) driven by lingering credit-risk fears around AI infrastructure firm CoreWeave.

Secondary-battery names (Samsung SDI, LG Energy Solution, Ecopro, Ecopro BM) surged 7–11% and led the rebound alongside autos, while large-cap semiconductors (Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix) posted only modest gains. Foreign and institutional investors turned net buyers intraday; retail sold into the rally.

The macro backdrop remains unresolved: US 10-year yields near 5.1% (a ~25-year high area touched 5.36% on 10/7) and AI-debt stress at CoreWeave continue to weigh on sentiment. Meta's earnings on 10/21 (US time) are widely seen as the next catalyst for AI-trade risk appetite. Figures follow the Korean original.

5다음 거래일(10/20) 관전 포인트 美 빅테크 실적 주간

한 문장. 오늘의 +1.76% 반등은 코어위브 신용위험이 해소된 결과가 아니라 저가 매수세가 선반영한 기술적 되돌림에 가깝다 — 이번 주 美 빅테크 실적이 추세 지속 여부를 가를 분수령이다.
