🌐 English SummaryEN brief

Korea close — risk-off. The KOSPI fell 3.59% to 5,648.79 and the KOSDAQ dropped 4.08% to 704.39, erasing the prior session's "5,000-plus" rally in a single day.

The trigger was renewed worry over the profitability of U.S. big-tech AI spending, compounded by Apple's cautious earnings outlook, which pressured Asian markets broadly. Large-cap chipmakers led the slide, with Samsung Electronics down about 4% and SK hynix down about 5%.

Foreigners and institutions were net sellers in tandem while retail investors absorbed the supply. The next session hinges on the U.S. midterm outcome, big-tech earnings digestion, and whether semiconductor sentiment stabilizes.

Figures follow the Korean original.
