🌐 English SummaryEN brief

Alphabet (GOOGL) — Q3 2026 earnings review. Reported GAAP EPS was $2.12 versus a pre-print consensus of $3.11 (a −31.8% headline surprise), but the GAAP figure was weighed down by roughly $8.66bn of equity-investment losses and a ~$5.2bn EU fine; on an adjusted basis EPS was about $2.67 versus about $3.02 expected, a much smaller operational miss.

Revenue grew 25% year over year to $127.58bn — a slim ~0.5% shortfall versus consensus — led by Google Cloud up 103% to $30.7bn and Search up 12%. The data provider's captured "actual" revenue of $111.21bn does not match the reported total, so the −15.8% revenue surprise reflects a data-basis mismatch rather than a real miss.

Shares fell roughly 11% from the pre-print close to $288.49, with the market focused on negative free cash flow of about −$9.07bn and a sharp rise in long-term debt amid heavy AI capex. The next checkpoint is the Q4 print scheduled for 2026-02-02 and whether consensus estimates are revised after the call.

This is a lightweight review for information only and not investment advice. Figures follow the Korean original.
