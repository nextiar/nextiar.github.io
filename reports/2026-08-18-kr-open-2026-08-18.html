English summary (3-min read)
On the first session after Korea's Liberation Day long weekend (markets closed Aug 15 & 17), the KOSPI gapped up +2.15% to 7,127.77 at the open, reclaiming the 7,100 line for the first time in 17 trading days. The rally was led almost entirely by large-cap chips — SK Hynix (+7.5%) and Samsung Electronics (+4.0%) topped turnover — even though Wall Street fell for a second straight day overnight (S&P −0.2%, Nasdaq −0.3%, Dow −0.2%). The KOSDAQ diverged, opening +0.22% then turning negative, underscoring an extreme large-cap tilt. Leverage-ETF turnover and a sharp drop in the 2x-inverse ETF point to bullish retail positioning, building on strong foreign net buying (+₩1.8tn on Aug 14). The key question into the close is whether the gap-up holds; watch the 7,100 level, the KOSPI-vs-KOSDAQ divergence, and whether foreign buying is confirmed.
