English summary
The KOSPI rebounded +3.29% to 5,735.66 on Oct 21, 2026, clawing back almost all of the prior session's 3.73% slide. The trigger came overnight: U.S. AI-server maker Super Micro reported preliminary quarterly revenue of about $19bn (above the ~$15bn consensus), easing worries over AI-capex profitability and pulling buyers back into chips. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix both jumped around 5%, leading the advance and triggering a buy-side sidecar — the first in roughly two months — mid-morning. The recovery was concentrated in large-cap semiconductors: the KOSPI (+3.29%) far outpaced the KOSDAQ (+1.38%), and foreigners turned net buyers on the main board while still net-selling the KOSDAQ. The won firmed to 1,332.1 per dollar, a supportive backdrop for foreign flows. Figures follow the Korean original.
