The KOSPI rebounded +3.09% to 5,638.34 and the KOSDAQ rose +2.29% to 743.83 on Oct 27, 2026, fully reversing the prior session's drop. A program-buy sidecar was triggered at 10:13 a.m. as the index briefly cleared 5,700.

Two catalysts converged: Anthropic's IPO filing reignited the global AI rally, lifting semiconductors across Asia, while Korea's Q3 GDP surprised sharply to the upside at +1.0% QoQ (vs. the 0.3% forecast), driven by chip exports and a 4.1% jump in facilities investment. Real GDI rose 18.8% YoY, the fastest in roughly 49 years, though private consumption turned negative.

Leadership was concentrated in chips — SK Hynix +5.33%, Samsung Electro-Mechanics +7.51%, Samsung Electronics +4.22% — with soobujang and battery names also firm. Foreigners (+KRW 593bn) and institutions (+KRW 280bn) bought together while retail sold KRW 948bn. Defensives such as Samsung Biologics (−5.85%) lagged.
