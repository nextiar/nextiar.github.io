🌐 English SummaryEN brief

Wall Street roared back on Mon, Nov 23. The Nasdaq jumped ~6.5%, the S&P 500 ~4.6% and the Dow ~1,470 points (~3.0%) — the best day since April 2025 — after Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. Nvidia soared into the 30% range, adding more than $1tn in market value at its intraday peak.

Crucially, memory rallied alongside logic this time: Micron climbed +19.5% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose ~13%, with AMD +9%, Qualcomm +9.8% and Broadcom +5.7%. That differs from July's "logic up, memory down" divergence.

This was a relief bounce after a brutal week (S&P −8.6%, its worst since the 2020 COVID sell-off). Treasury yields rose (10Y ~4.93%, 2Y ~4.68%) and markets still price a ~98% chance of a 25bp Fed hike in December — a hawkish backdrop that keeps the rebound unconfirmed.

For Korea: memory strength is a direct read-through to Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, which had just plunged. The Bank of Korea's Nov 26 rate decision (hike expected) is the key domestic overhang.
