English summary

Wall Street sold off hard on Fri Oct 30, 2026, closing out October's final session with the Nasdaq Composite down 2.86% (−747p) to 25,437 — its worst month since March 2025 — the S&P 500 off 1.82% to 7,580, and the Dow down 1.09% to 51,770. Earnings from Amazon and Alphabet rekindled fears over how much Big Tech is spending on AI, with Amazon's trailing free cash flow swinging to a roughly $42bn outflow as capex doubled year-over-year.

Semiconductors were the epicenter: the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) plunged 7.22% as Intel sank about 28% on weak Q4 guidance, dragging logic and foundry names, while memory (Micron) also fell — a reminder to read chips by sub-sector (logic vs. memory vs. equipment) rather than as one trade. The 10-year Treasury yield eased to roughly 4.93% at the close (FRED daily 4.98%), but the policy backdrop stays hawkish: Q3 GDP ran +3.4% and September core PCE held at +3.1% y/y, with the Fed on hold at 3.75–4.00% and markets still pricing a possible December hike.

Korea had already dropped on Friday — KOSPI −3.5% with an 11-session-first sell-side sidecar and ~KRW 1.6tn of foreign net selling, as Samsung Electronics fell ~6% and SK hynix ~8% on the "Alphabet shock" during Asian hours. The overnight U.S. close extends the AI-capex scare into Monday's (Nov 2) open; watch memory read-through, the logic/memory/equipment split, foreign flows despite a firmer won, and elevated U.S. yields. 왜 중요한가. 최근 한국 증시를 흔든 핵심이 바로 이 AI 투자 지속성·메모리 밸류에이션 논쟁이다. 지난 '애플·마이크론 다이버전스' 국면과 달리, 이번엔 로직·파운드리(인텔·AMD·엔비디아)와 메모리(마이크론)가 함께 밀렸다. 즉 "AI 로직만 강하고 메모리만 약하다"는 구분이 이번엔 통하지 않았고, AI 설비투자 전반의 회수 시점에 대한 의구심이 섹터를 한꺼번에 눌렀다. 마이크론 ≈−5%는 오늘(월) 삼성전자·SK하이닉스에 직접적인 심리적 read-through가 되고, 인텔 파운드리 쇼크는 국내 파운드리·장비 밸류체인에도 간접 신호다.
