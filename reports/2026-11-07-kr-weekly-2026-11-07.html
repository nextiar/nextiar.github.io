🌐 English SummaryEN brief

A "Black Friday" week. The KOSPI began the week strongly — up 2.24% to 5,859.08 on Monday, reclaiming the 5,800 line — but reversed violently late in the week, plunging 5.28% on Thursday and 6.02% on Friday to close at 5,059.69. The KOSDAQ fell to 644.27. Week-on-week (vs the prior Friday's close), the KOSPI lost 11.7% and the KOSDAQ 11.1%.

The trigger was the U.S. midterm "blue wave": a Democratic sweep of Congress stoked fears of tighter oversight of AI data-center buildout and energy use, sinking U.S. AI and tech names. Korea's chipmakers bore the brunt — over Thursday and Friday, Samsung Electronics fell 13.44% and SK Hynix 18.91%.

Flows were the story: foreigners dumped KRW 2.77tn on the main board Friday alone, absorbed by institutions (+1.70tn) and retail (+0.60tn). Intraday the KOSPI sank to 4,911, triggering a circuit breaker and a sell sidecar.

Next week hinges on whether bargain-buying holds the 5,000 line, on the direction of foreign flows, and on major U.S. AI events (Nvidia, CoreWeave).

Figures follow the Korean original.
