🌐 English SummaryEN brief

Korea close — risk-off. The KOSPI opened higher but reversed to close down 3.59% (−146.50 points) at 5,100.50, barely holding the 5,100 line, while the KOSDAQ fell a steeper 4.86%. Memory heavyweights led the slide: SK hynix −7.3% and Samsung Electronics −4.4%.

The trigger was caution ahead of an "AI big week" — Nvidia's earnings and Anthropic-related chip supply worries, plus a Fed warning on AI-investment risk. The sell-off was region-wide, with Japan's Nikkei dropping below 60,000 and Taiwan's index down around 3% on TSMC weakness.

Foreign investors drove the fall, net-selling over KRW 1tn on the main board (reports ranged from ~KRW 1.4tn intraday to ~KRW 1.6tn at the close), while retail buying cushioned part of the drop. The won stayed firm despite the equity rout, trading in the low-to-mid 1,330s.

Next session, watch whether memory names stabilize around Nvidia's results and whether foreigners slow their selling.

한 문장. 오늘 급락은 엔비디아 실적을 앞둔 'AI 빅위크' 경계가 반도체 대형주 투매로 터진 결과로, 다음 거래일 방향은 간밤 미국 반도체·엔비디아 관련 뉴스 흐름과 외국인 매도 지속 여부가 가른다.
