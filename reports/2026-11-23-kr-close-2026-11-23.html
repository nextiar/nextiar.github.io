English summary
Korea's KOSPI fell 3.31% to 4,708.54 and the KOSDAQ dropped 1.58% to 635.45 on Monday, as Friday's U.S. AI-and-chip rout (S&P 500 −3.7%, into correction) spilled over into Asia. Intraday the KOSPI sank as much as ~5.8% below Friday's close, breaching 4,600 and triggering a sell-side sidecar for a second straight session, before recouping much of the loss into the close. Banks, pharma and shipbuilders led the declines; index heavyweights Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix slipped roughly 2–3%. Retail investors were net sellers, while the direction of foreign and institutional flows was reported inconsistently across outlets and is left unconfirmed. The government said it is preparing to activate a stock-market stabilization fund, will lift the daily buyback-order cap from the 24th for three months, and will intensify monitoring of illegal short-selling.
