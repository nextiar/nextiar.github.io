English summary

Seoul opened sharply lower on Fri Oct 30, 2026, extending the prior session's slide. The KOSPI200 proxy (KODEX 200 ETF) was down about −4.1% in the first 30 minutes, while the broad KOSPI fell into the −3% range and gave back the 6,000 level; the KOSDAQ was down roughly −3.0%.

The trigger was overnight New York, where Alphabet fell −12.64% as "does AI capex actually earn money?" doubts returned, with Amazon and Intel wobbling further after hours. That read-through hit Korea's large-cap chips hardest — SK Hynix around −7%, Samsung Electronics around −4%, Samsung Electro-Mechanics around −7% intraday.

Flows were defensive: inverse and 2x-inverse ETFs climbed the turnover leaderboard, and foreigners and institutions were both net sellers early. The won weakened to 1,336.3 vs the dollar, adding to the pressure on foreign flows.

Watch SK Hynix / Samsung's first-hour recovery or breakdown, whether battery/defensive names hold up against the chip-led decline, and the 5,700 KOSPI zone as a near-term pivot. All figures are intraday as of ~09:30 KST and will be finalized in the 16:00 close wrap. Figures follow the Korean original.
