Foreign investors sold over KRW 3tn on the KOSPI (preliminary, ~15:35), while retail (≈+KRW 2.27tn) and institutions (≈+KRW 0.85tn) absorbed the selling. A single-sector shock in semiconductors pulled the whole index down.
