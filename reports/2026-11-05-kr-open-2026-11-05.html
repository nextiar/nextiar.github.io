The KOSPI opened Nov 5, 2026 at 5,580.09, down 1.82% (−103.71 pts), and widened its loss to about −2.29% (5,553.39) by 9:08 a.m. — a "wrong-way" gap down even though Wall Street surged overnight (Dow +3.03%). The offset was a rotation that mirrored the overnight U.S. move: Hyundai Motor +6–7%, Celltrion +7%, Samsung Biologics +4.6%, plus strength in banks, pharma and utilities. Foreign investors were reported to be net sellers for a fifth straight session into the open.
