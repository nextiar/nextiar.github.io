US close, Fri Nov 13, 2026. The Dow edged up +0.10% to 52,347.55 while the S&P 500 fell −0.40% (7,561.67) and the Nasdaq Composite dropped −0.91% (25,136.27), as the abrupt resignation of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang unwound the week's AI-chip rally. All three indexes still finished the week higher.

Logic/GPU names led the slide — AMD −9% (−10%+ intraday), Broadcom −6%, Intel −5%, Nvidia −4% — and the VanEck Semiconductor ETF lost 5.3%; memory (Micron −3.96%) fell less but in the same direction.

The bigger macro story was October PPI at +6.5% year over year, the highest in about four years. The 10-year Treasury yield pushed above 5%, and markets fully price a December Fed hike, pressuring high-multiple growth and semis.

Next watch: how Korea's memory complex (Samsung, SK hynix) absorbs the Nvidia/Micron read-through, and whether 5% US yields drive a hawkish repricing. Figures follow the Korean original.

5오늘(11/14) 국내 장 시사점 변동성 경계

한 문장. 간밤 뉴욕은 엔비디아 CEO 사임發 AI칩 되돌림과 PPI 6.5%·국채 5% 돌파라는 두 악재가 겹쳤다 — 오늘 국내 장은 이 충격을 삼성전자·SK하이닉스 메모리 밸류체인이 얼마나 흡수하느냐, 그리고 금리 급등이 고밸류 성장주 할인으로 번지느냐가 방향을 가른다.
