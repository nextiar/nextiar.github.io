🌐 English SummaryEN brief

NVIDIA (NVDA) — earnings review, fiscal Q3 2027 (quarter ended Oct 25, 2026). Non-GAAP diluted EPS of $2.68 beat the pre-print consensus baseline of $2.525 by 6.1%, and revenue of $115.844B beat the $111.282B consensus by 4.1%, up 103.2% YoY and 20.4% QoQ. Data Center revenue was $107.6B, 92.9% of the total.

Management guided fiscal Q4 revenue to $130.0B, plus or minus 3% (including an extra week and assuming no Data Center compute revenue from China), with gross margin guided to 71.0–72.0%, about 3 points below Q3's 74.9%. It was the first report since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on Nov 12; interim CEO Colette Kress said Vera Rubin is "off to an explosive start."

Shares rose about 4.6% in extended trading to $162.58 from the $155.48 pre-print close. A same-basis post-print consensus revision is not yet observable. Next checkpoint: the fiscal Q4 report around Feb 23, 2027, where the gross-margin trough and the Vera Rubin ramp are the key watch items.

Figures follow the Korean original.
