🌐 English SummaryEN brief

KOSPI −6.02% at 5,059.69; KOSDAQ −5.03% at 644.27. Korean stocks fell for a second straight day. The KOSPI sank as much as 8.52% intraday to 4,924.61, tripping the year's 11th circuit breaker, before trimming losses to hold the 5,000 line into the close.

The trigger was Wall Street's overnight rout: after the Democratic midterm sweep stoked fears of AI regulation and slowing data-center capex, the Nasdaq dropped 5.52% with Nvidia −9% and AMD −17%, erasing roughly 3,400 trillion won of market value in a day. That shock passed straight through to Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix.

Foreigners sold about KRW 3.4tn in the main board, with an estimated KRW 2.7tn concentrated in semiconductors, while defensive biotech (Samsung Biologics roughly flat) held up relatively better.

Key watch for the next session: whether Wall Street rebounds overnight and whether foreign selling in chips subsides. Figures follow the Korean original.

5내일(다음 거래일) 관전 포인트 변동성 극대

한 문장. 이틀 연속 5~6%대 폭락으로 코스피가 5,000선, 코스닥이 640선을 턱걸이로 지킨 가운데, 간밤 뉴욕이 반등하는지와 외국인의 반도체 매도가 멈추는지가 다음 거래일 방향을 가른다.
