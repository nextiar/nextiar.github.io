English summary ▾

U.S. stocks posted their worst week since June: the S&P 500 fell 2.7% to 7,376.86 and the Nasdaq dropped 3.4% to 24,579.42. The catalyst was the Nov. 3 midterms, where a Democratic sweep of Congress sparked fears of tighter AI data-center regulation. That triggered Thursday's rout — the worst single day since April 2025, with the Nasdaq down 5.5% into correction territory and the VIX spiking 70% to 28.18, as the Magnificent Seven shed over $1 trillion in a day.

The shock hit export-driven Korea harder: foreign investors dumped more than 10 trillion won of KOSPI shares (7.6 trillion in Samsung and SK Hynix alone), sending the KOSPI down 11.7% on the week and triggering a circuit breaker on Friday as it barely held the 5,000 level. A stronger-than-expected October U.S. jobs report (165k vs. 59k est., 4.1% unemployment) lifted Wall Street modestly on Friday but trimmed rate-cut bets. Next week's October CPI (Nov. 10) and PPI (Nov. 13), plus Cisco and Applied Materials earnings, are the key watch points. Figures follow the Korean original.

4승자와 패자 — 반도체가 진앙, 한국이 최대 피해 반도체 집중타

이번 주는 '올라간 섹터'를 찾기 어려운 전면 리스크오프였다. 낙폭은 AI·반도체·데이터센터에 집중됐고, 그 충격파가 메모리 수출국 한국을 가장 세게 때렸다.
