English summary (for non-Korean readers)
Korea's KOSPI opened sharply higher — up over 2% and touching 5,500 intraday — but reversed to close down 0.98% at 5,290.53 as foreign investors turned net sellers (roughly 550 billion won sold on the main board) around President Lee's capital-market reform roundtable. Semiconductors led the give-back: SK Hynix and other large caps surrendered early gains after Nvidia's overnight shock. The KOSDAQ held up better, closing up 0.45% at 699.66 (below 700) on institutional buying of about 126 billion won. Overnight on Wall Street, the three major indices fell (Nasdaq −0.39%) after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation, with NVDA −4.34% while AMD and Broadcom rallied. The won firmed modestly to 1,336.70 per dollar. Next session's focus: whether foreigners keep selling, how the semis digest Nvidia's leadership gap, and the policy momentum from the reform roundtable. Figures follow the Korean original.
