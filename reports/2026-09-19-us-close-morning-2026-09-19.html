English summary
Wall Street closed mixed on Friday, Sept 18, 2026, as markets digested the Fed's first rate hike in over three years (Sept 16, to 3.75–4.00%). The 10-year Treasury yield pushed toward 5%, yet the Nasdaq still rose +0.39% and the S&P 500 gained +0.17%, while the Dow slipped −0.18% (down ~1.5% for the week). Semiconductors were the engine — chips "kept running even with the 10-year near 5%," underpinned by AI-demand optimism (Nvidia guiding to ~2x chip volume next year; Broadcom's AI revenue surging). It was quarterly triple-witching (~$7T of options expiring). Korea had already taken the hike as "uncertainty resolved": on Friday KOSPI jumped +2.66% and foreigners bought for the first time in eight sessions, led by Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. The next Korean session (Monday) hinges on whether AI/semis can keep outrunning higher rates and a weaker won. Figures follow the Korean original.

5다음 국내 장(월) 시사점 고금리 vs AI 힘겨루기

한 문장. 간밤 뉴욕은 금리 인상 충격을 반도체로 상쇄했고, 국내는 이미 9/18에 '불확실성 해소'로 +2.66% 반등했다 — 다음 국내 장(월)의 관건은 이 AI·반도체 모멘텀이 고금리·강달러를 계속 이겨낼지다.
