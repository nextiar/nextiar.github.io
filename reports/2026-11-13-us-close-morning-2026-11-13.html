English summary (3-sentence abstract)

On Thursday, Nov 12, 2026, Wall Street closed modestly lower (S&P 500 −0.35% to 7,592.40; Nasdaq −0.39%; Dow −0.36%) — a notably shallow drop given the day's headline: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang abruptly resigned, with CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO. Nvidia fell as much as 13% intraday before closing around $164 (roughly −5%), while buyers rotated into rivals Broadcom (+~5%) and AMD; memory maker Micron slipped −2.7%.

The heavier weight on the tape was rates: the 10-year Treasury yield jumped back to about 5.11% (+~11bp) as a hotter-than-expected CPI (Nov 10) and hawkish Fed commentary pushed December rate-hike odds to roughly 100%. For Korea on Nov 13, the read-through is mixed — the Nvidia leadership shock is now confirmed (not just a rumor as during Thursday's Seoul session), but the structural HBM supercycle (SK Hynix/Samsung) and the rival-rotation cut both ways.
