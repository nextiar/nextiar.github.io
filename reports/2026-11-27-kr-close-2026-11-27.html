KOSPI +4.34% to 4,915.68, KOSDAQ +8.13% to 708.99. Korean equities snapped back sharply on Nov 27, reclaiming the 4,900 and 700 levels that had broken down the prior week on AI/semiconductor fears. The KOSDAQ posted its third-largest single-day gain of the year and triggered a buy-side sidecar.

Foreign investors returned as net buyers of KRW 1.13tn on the main board (institutions +KRW 0.24tn) while retail sold KRW 1.77tn. Drivers were hopes for an enlarged market-stabilization fund, an unusual intraday pledge of market support from Chinese authorities, and a sense that the NVIDIA-led AI correction is fading.

Much of the move looks like a mean-reversion bounce off oversold levels. The key swing factors are the durability of foreign buying, concrete stabilization measures, and next week's US PCE inflation print. The won firmed to 1,337.9 per dollar.
